# SAMPLE SHIFT // VR

WebXR warehouse-club horror for Quest 3 (and desktop). 11:53 PM closing shift:
collect the six sample cups still out on the floor while the cardless member by
the freezer wall drifts closer with every cup.

- Quest 3: left stick move, right stick snap turn, point + trigger to collect
- Desktop: WASD move, drag to look, click a cup to collect
- Single-file three.js (r170, vendored), no build step

Live: https://jacobegarcia.github.io/vr-costco/
